COMPLAIN MANAGEMENT MODULE  (HELP DESK)  1. Purpose & Vision  The   Complaint Management (Help Desk) Module   enables any community member, staff, or  authorized third-party vendor to   log issues ,   track progress ,   ensure accountability , and  guarantee timely resolution   with   automated escalation   and   transparent reporting .  This module acts as the   operational backbone   for community service quality.  2. Who Can Raise Complaints (Universal Access Model)  Primary Users     Residents / Members     Office Bearers (President, Secretary, Treasurer)     Management Office Staff     Mosque / Madrasah Admin (if applicable)     Mall / Facility Visitors (controlled access)     Contractors & Vendors (limited scope)  Secondary / Oversight Users     Department Heads     Facility Managers     Community Administrators     Auditors / Inspectors (read-only)     Super Admin (system-wide)  3. Complaint Types & Classification Engine  A. Complaint Categories (Configurable)     Maintenance (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC)     Security & Safety     Cleanliness & Hygiene     Parking Issues

   Water / Gas / Power     Noise / Nuisance     Staff Misconduct     Vendor Issues     Financial / Billing     Facility Damage     IT / Digital Services     Suggestions / Feedback     Emergency Complaints (High Priority)  B. Sub-Categories  Example:     Maintenance → Electrical → Street Lights     Security → Access Control → Unauthorized Entry  (Admin can   add / edit / disable   categories)  4. Complaint Registration (Smart Intake System)  Multiple Channels     Mobile App     Web Portal     Kiosk (Optional)     Admin Manual Entry     QR-based Complaint Submission (Location-aware)  Complaint Form Fields     Complaint Title     Detailed Description (Rich Text)     Category & Sub-Category     Priority (Auto + Manual Override)     Location (Tower, Floor, Unit, Area)     Attachments:  o   Images  o   Videos  o   Voice Notes  o   Documents (PDF, DOC)     Preferred Contact Method     Emergency Flag (Yes/No)

AI-Assisted Enhancements (Optional)     Auto-suggest category from text     Duplicate complaint detection     Severity prediction  5. Complaint Lifecycle & Status Flow  Standard Workflow  1.   New  2.   Acknowledged  3.   Assigned  4.   In Progress  5.   On Hold (with reason)  6.   Resolved  7.   Closed  8.   Reopened (if not satisfied)  9.   Escalated (parallel state)  Each status change is:     Time-stamped     Logged     Visible to the complainant  6. Assignment & Workforce Management  Assignment Modes     Manual Assignment     Auto-Assignment (Rule Based)  o   Category → Department  o   Location → Staff  o   Workload balancing     Vendor Assignment (if outsourced)  Staff Roster Integration     Working hours     Off-days

   Skill mapping     Emergency duty staff  7. SLA & Escalation Matrix (Critical Feature)  SLA Definition     Category-wise SLA     Priority-based SLA     Emergency override SLAs  Escalation Levels     Level 1: Assigned Staff     Level 2: Supervisor     Level 3: Department Head     Level 4: Community Admin     Level 5: Governing Body  Auto Escalation Triggers     No acknowledgment     SLA breach     Repeated reopening     High-risk complaint  Escalation via:     App Notification     Email     SMS     WhatsApp (Optional)  8. Resolution Management  Resolution Capture     Resolution Notes (mandatory)     Before / After Images     Cost Incurred (optional)     Vendor Invoice Upload

   Parts Used / Materials  Closure Controls     Complainant confirmation     Auto-close after X days     Reopen window configurable  9. Feedback, Rating & Accountability     Star Rating (1–5)     Comment box     Staff performance tagging     Vendor performance impact     Anonymous feedback option  Low ratings automatically flag:     Supervisor review     Training needs     Vendor warnings  10. Advanced Dashboards & KPIs  Real-Time Dashboards     Open vs Closed Complaints     SLA Compliance %     Average Closure Time     Escalation Heatmap     Department Performance  Reports (Exportable)     Category-wise     Tower / Block-wise     Staff-wise     Vendor-wise     Time-based (Daily / Monthly / Annual)     Defaulter Complaints (Repeated Issues)

11. Cost & Budget Tracking (Optional but Powerful)     Complaint-wise cost     Department budgets     Vendor billing linkage     Maintenance cost trends     Capital vs Operational expense tagging  12. Communication & Notifications     In-app chat per complaint     Admin notes (internal only)     Broadcast updates (linked with Digital Notice Board)     Auto reminders     Multi-language support  13. Audit Trail & Compliance     Immutable activity logs     User actions tracking     SLA breach history     Complaint deletion control     Legal / inspection ready reports  14. Security & Access Control     Role-based access     Complaint visibility rules     Sensitive complaint masking     Data encryption     GDPR-like consent controls (future-proof)  15. AI & Automation (Advanced Tier)

   Predictive issue hotspots     Preventive maintenance alerts     Auto-recommend solutions     Chatbot for complaint logging     Sentiment analysis on feedback  16. Integration Capabilities     Facility Management Module     Project Management Module     Digital Notice Board     Accounting & Billing     IoT Sensors (Water, Power, Elevators)     CCTV snapshot linkage  17. Mobile-First UX Features     One-tap complaint     Location auto-detect     Offline submission     Push notifications     Status timeline view  18. Why This Module Is STRONG  ✔   Works for   ANY community type  ✔   Scales from   small societies to mega organizations  ✔   Ensures   transparency + accountability  ✔   Reduces complaint resolution time  ✔   Improves community trust

USER STORIES – COMPLAINT  MANAGEMENT / HELP DESK MODULE  USER ROLES COVERED  1.   Community Member / Resident  2.   Staff / Service Personnel  3.   Vendor / Contractor  4.   Department Supervisor  5.   Community Admin / Management Office  6.   Governing Body / Committee Member  7.   Super Admin (Platform Owner / Oversight)  8.   Auditor / Inspector (Read-Only)  1. COMMUNITY MEMBER / RESIDENT  USER STORIES  1.1 Raise a Complaint  As a Community Member  I want to log a complaint easily  So that my issue is officially recorded and resolved.  Acceptance Criteria     Can select category & sub-category     Can attach photos, videos, voice notes     Can mark urgency     Can auto-detect location or select manually     Complaint ID generated instantly

1.2 Track Complaint Status  As a Community Member  I want to track the real-time status of my complaint  So that I know what is happening without calling the office.  Acceptance Criteria     Status timeline visible     Assigned staff/vendor visible     SLA countdown visible     Escalation status shown  1.3 Communicate on Complaint  As a Community Member  I want to add comments or additional evidence  So that the resolution is accurate.  1.4 Escalate Automatically  As a Community Member  I want my complaint escalated automatically if delayed  So that I don’t have to chase authorities.  1.5 Reopen or Confirm Closure  As a Community Member  I want to confirm satisfaction or reopen the complaint  So unresolved issues are not closed unfairly.  1.6 Rate Resolution  As a Community Member  I want to rate the service  So good or bad performance is recorded.

2. STAFF / SERVICE PERSONNEL USER  STORIES  2.1 View Assigned Complaints  As a Staff Member  I want to see only my assigned complaints  So I can focus on my responsibilities.  2.2 Update Status  As a Staff Member  I want to update progress  So stakeholders stay informed.  2.3 Add Resolution Evidence  As a Staff Member  I want to upload before/after images and notes  So work done is verifiable.  2.4 Request Support or Extension  As a Staff Member  I want to request additional time or resources  So SLA exceptions are documented.

3. VENDOR / CONTRACTOR USER  STORIES  3.1 Receive Assigned Tickets  As a Vendor  I want to receive complaints assigned to my contract  So I can act within scope.  3.2 Upload Completion Proof  As a Vendor  I want to upload invoices, images, and work notes  So payments and audits are smooth.  3.3 View Performance Feedback  As a Vendor  I want to see feedback related to my work  So I can improve service quality.  4. DEPARTMENT SUPERVISOR USER  STORIES  4.1 Monitor Department Complaints  As a Supervisor  I want to monitor all complaints under my department  So SLA compliance is maintained.

4.2 Reassign or Escalate  As a Supervisor  I want to reassign complaints or escalate them  So bottlenecks are resolved quickly.  4.3 Review Low Ratings  As a Supervisor  I want to review low-rated complaints  So corrective actions can be taken.  5. COMMUNITY ADMIN /  MANAGEMENT OFFICE USER STORIES  5.1 Configure Complaint Categories  As a Community Admin  I want to define complaint categories and SLAs  So the system matches real operations.  5.2 Auto-Assignment Rules  As a Community Admin  I want to configure auto-assignment rules  So complaints reach the right team instantly.  5.3 SLA & Escalation Control  As a Community Admin  I want to define escalation matrices  So accountability is enforced without manual follow-ups.

5.4 Override & Emergency Handling  As a Community Admin  I want to override assignments during emergencies  So critical issues are resolved immediately.  5.5 Dashboard & Reporting  As a Community Admin  I want real-time dashboards and reports  So I can manage performance and planning.  6. GOVERNING BODY / COMMITTEE  MEMBER USER STORIES  6.1 Oversight Dashboard  As a Committee Member  I want a summarized complaint dashboard  So governance decisions are data-driven.  6.2 Escalation Visibility  As a Committee Member  I want to see escalated and repeated complaints  So systemic issues are addressed.  6.3 Policy Review Inputs  As a Committee Member  I want complaint trends  So policies and budgets can be improved.

7. SUPER ADMIN USER STORIES  7.1 Multi-Community Oversight  As a Super Admin  I want to view complaint performance across communities  So platform quality is maintained.  7.2 Role & Permission Control  As a Super Admin  I want granular permission control  So access is secure and compliant.  7.3 System Audit & Logs  As a Super Admin  I want immutable logs  So the system is audit-ready.  8. AUDITOR / INSPECTOR USER  STORIES  8.1 Read-Only Access  As an Auditor  I want read-only access to complaint records  So inspections are transparent.  8.2 Export Reports  As an Auditor  I want exportable reports  So compliance verification is easy.

CROSS-ROLE ADVANCED USER  STORIES (AI & AUTOMATION)  A1. Duplicate Detection  As the System  I want to detect duplicate complaints  So workload is reduced.  A2. Predictive Alerts  As Management  I want predictive alerts on recurring issues  So preventive action can be taken.  A3. Chatbot Intake  As a User  I want to log complaints via chatbot  So access is frictionless.

Digital Notice Board — Screen-by-Screen Developer Specs  (Web Portal + Mobile)

## 0) Global Definitions (Applies to All Screens)  ### 0.1 Actors / Roles  - **Super Admin** (platform owner)  - **Community Admin** (management office)  - **Committee Member** (approver/publisher depending RBAC)  - **Staff** (security/maintenance/admin office; limited)  - **Resident** (Owner/Tenant)  - **Vendor/Contractor** (limited, optional)  - **Guest/Temporary** (optional)  ### 0.2 Announcement Types  - **General** (visible to all within community scope)  - **Targeted** (subset by targeting rules)  - **Restricted** (staff/committee-only or specific roles)  - **Emergency** (override rules; forced prominence; special notification behavior)  ### 0.3 Priority Levels (UI badges + ordering)  - **Normal**  - **High**  - **Urgent** (sticky/pin behavior allowed)  - **Emergency** (always top; may use fullscreen takeover)  ### 0.4 Categories (configurable per community)  - Community Updates, Maintenance/Utilities, Events, Security Alerts, Financial/Dues, Admin/Policy, Lost  & Found, Religious/Cultural (optional)

### 0.5 Core Lifecycle / Status Model  ### 0.6 Targeting Model (Rules)  Target rules are evaluated as **AND of groups**, with **OR within selections**:  - Community -> Block/Tower -> Floor -> Unit  - Role: Owner/Tenant/Both, Committee, Staff categories  - Individuals  - Custom groups/tags (e.g., Volunteers)  - Optional: age group, gender (only if legally/ethically enabled)  **Emergency**: may ignore targeting and push to “All reachable users”, but still respects “community  boundary”.

### 0.7 Read vs Acknowledgement  - **Read/Seen**: system marks when a user opens the announcement detail (or scroll threshold; define  below).  - **Acknowledged**: explicit action (“Mark as read” / “I have read & understood”).  - Emergency/Urgent can require acknowledgement.  **Default rule:**  - Read = opening detail (and content loaded) + 2s dwell, or scroll \(\ge 40\%\) for long content.  - Acknowledge = button tap + confirmation.  ### 0.8 Notifications (Channels + Quiet Hours)  Channels:  - In-app, Push (mobile), Email (optional), SMS (urgent/emergency), WhatsApp (future)  Quiet Hours:  - Normal/High: respect quiet hours  - Urgent: configurable (respect by default)  - Emergency: **override quiet hours** if enabled  ### 0.9 Attachments Rules  - Types: PDF, JPG/PNG, DOC/DOCX, XLS/XLSX, MP4 (admin-controlled), audio optional  - Per-file size limit, total per announcement limit  - Download permission flags:  - View-only (stream/preview)  - Download allowed  - Watermark on download (optional)  ### 0.10 Multi-language  - Per user: preferred language  - Per announcement:  - Single language OR  - Multi-language variants (e.g., EN/UR/AR)  - If missing variant, fallback order: user pref → community default → first available.  ---
